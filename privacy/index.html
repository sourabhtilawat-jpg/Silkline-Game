Privacy Policy

Information we collect

None. Silkline does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and the developer receives no data about you or how you play.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (cleared missions, unlocked cities and items) and your settings (language, graphics quality, sound) in your device's local storage so you can continue where you left off. This data is never sent to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Network use

The game runs offline. When your device is online, it may download its display fonts from Google Fonts. As with any web request, Google receives your device's IP address to deliver the font files; see Google's Privacy Policy. Silkline sends no other network requests and shares no game data.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only to load fonts, as described above.
  • Vibrate: used for haptic feedback during fights.

Silkline does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage outside its own app data.

Third parties

Silkline contains no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs. The app is distributed through Google Play, which handles downloads and updates under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Children

Silkline is an action game intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts collecting data, for example to add online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Silkline can be sent to the developer, Sourabh Tilawat, at the contact email listed on Silkline's Google Play store page.
